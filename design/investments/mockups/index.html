Design notes

  • Colours and type are the suite's own tokens. Two proposed new tokens: gain green #2d5a3d and loss brick #a8441f, both distinct from the oxblood used for actions and alerts.
  • No web fonts are loaded, as in the live suite, so serif and sans fall back to fonts already on the device.
  • Charts are inline SVG with no library. Every chart distinguishes lines by pattern as well as shade.
