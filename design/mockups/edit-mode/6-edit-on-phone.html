A. Arbitrability

1. FRAUD

(1)
Mere, but not serious, allegations of fraud arbitrable - (a) A. Ayyasamy [(2016) 10 SCC 386] ; (b) Ameet Lalchand Shah [(2018) 15 SCC 678] @ para 22
(i)
Allegation must go to the very root of the contract - Vidya Drolia [(2021) 2 SCC 1]
(2)
Serious issue of fraud involving criminal wrongdoing not arbitrable - N. Radhakrishnan [(2010) 1 SCC 72]
(3)
Cannot file S. 11 when allegation that agreement procured by fraud - India Household [(2007) 5 SCC 510] For review

2. GENERAL

(1)
Rights in rem not arbitrable. Provides non-exhaustive list - Booz Allen [(2011) 5 SCC 532]
(2)
Issue of Limitation of the claim can be decided by the arbitrator - BSNL v. Nortel Networks (2021) 5 SCC 738 @ paras 40-47 & 53 [Overruled]
