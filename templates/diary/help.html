Help

This page explains the everyday tasks in plain words. Nothing here needs any technical knowledge. Use the Home button (the little house, top right) at any time to start again. The magnifier beside it is on every page and opens a search bar (on the Search page it jumps to the search box). The keyboard shortcuts are ⌥F for Search and ⌥H for Home (Option on a Mac; Alt on Windows; on Windows, Alt+F may also try to open the browser menu, so use Ctrl+Alt+F if so). Search can show results as cards or, when it covers the whole diary, "By Subject" with the Subjects on the left; "Search headers" looks only in Subject, Topic, Sub-topic and Section names.

Reading and finding things

On Home press the diary button. The Subjects page lists your Subjects. Press a Subject to read it. On a page showing just one Topic or Sub-topic, the two arrows beside its heading go to the previous and next one (from the last Sub-topic of a Topic, on to the next Topic). When a page reloads (after a change, Undo, or a phone reloading the tab) it opens where you were, not at the top. Use Search (top bar) to find words, a citation such as 2018 9 SCC 49, or an exact phrase in quotation marks. The ticks in Search (Citations, Flags, Headers) are remembered by this browser; History, at the start of that line, lists your recent searches. The Flags tab in the top bar (the flag) lists every flagged Item and Section, or one kind of flag, grouped by Subject, and searches the text beside the flags; its Hyperlinks page lists the citations that have a link, or those that do not yet. Group by Subject, beside the number of results, shows the results grouped under their Subjects or in one list; switching it does not change the search.

Editing

Only Editors can edit. Press the Edit link (the pencil) to switch editing on; the bar across the top says Editing. Every row then has two buttons: + Add and Edit. Change the text, press Save, and it is kept. An Item or a Section can be given a Flag (Pending, Overruled, Doubted or Other, chosen from a list, with text beside it if you like) as it is added, the same as when it is edited, on a phone as well. In Move to, press the arrow beside a Subject or Topic to open it (Expand all opens every one). Edit, then More, also has Promote to ... (Section to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Topic, Topic to Subject) and Demote to ... (Subject to Topic, Topic to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Section). A page says what will happen first, and Undo reverses it. A citation can carry a Link (a web address starting with https://), kept exactly as you type it; in the text the citation is then underlined and opens the link in a new tab. The chain-link icon in the top bar turns links off and on, without reloading the page. Press Done when you have finished. If you only want to read, leave Edit off.

If someone else changed the same record first, nothing is lost: you see both versions side by side and choose Keep mine, Keep theirs or Edit and merge. Every saved version stays in History (Edit, then More, then History) and can be brought back.

Trash and Undo

Deleting never removes anything at once. It moves the record, with what is under it, to the Trash. Open Trash from the top bar and press Restore to bring it back in its old place. Only an explicit Delete permanently removes it for good. While editing, the Undo button reverses your last action.

Search

Type a word or part of a word. Capital letters and punctuation do not matter. Put exact phrases in quotation marks. You can narrow a search by Subject and see the results in context.

Search understands a few simple operators (the Search tips panel under the search box lists them):

  • fraud forgery: both words (AND is the default).
  • fraud OR forgery: either word. Write OR, AND and NOT in capital letters.
  • fraud -criminal or fraud NOT criminal: leaves out results that have the word.
  • "scc 49": the exact phrase. (fraud OR forgery) -criminal: brackets group words.
  • arbitra*: words that start with these letters.
  • SCC 2016: finds citations holding those words and numbers.
  • subject:Arbitration fraud: only in Subjects with that name (subject:"Civil Procedure" for several words).
  • flag:pending, flag:review (overruled or doubted) or flag:other: only flagged records of that kind.

If a search cannot be understood (for example a bracket that is never closed), it is run as plain words and a short note says so. Words you left out are not highlighted.

Settings

There are three places for settings. My settings (click your name at the top right) holds what is only yours: your email address (used by Forgot my password on the login page, where you type your username), Change my password, what opens after you log in, justified text, and your own {{ diary_name }} choices (opening page, how numbers look, the date in Word file names). Settings (the gear in the diary) opens the {{ diary_name }} settings that everyone using the profile shares: Reading (the name shown at the top and whether a click on a Subject opens the whole Subject) and Data (the Word export file name, and for an Admin the Word import and the Offline link). Only Editors can change them; Readers can look. Each section has its own Save, and a note warns you about unsaved changes. History in the diary menu lists your own changes, newest first. Whether the header bar shows icons or words is set by an Admin for everyone, on Admins > Appearance. Tick Remember me when logging in to stay signed in for 30 days on your own device.

Profiles and users

A profile is a separate notebook of diary entries. A person may be given one or several profiles, as Reader or Editor in each. With several, the small arrow next to the name at the top of the diary (or the buttons on the Home page) switches between them; the one used last opens next time. The Admin makes profiles under Admins, tab Profiles, and, on its Users tab, adds people, changes passwords and chooses their profiles and roles. A deleted user can be restored from Deleted users on that tab for 30 days. Roles: Reader reads only, Editor reads and edits, Admin does everything including users and backups. If you see "No profile assigned", ask the Admin.

Backups and restore

The site saves a backup every night and keeps them for at least 30 days. The Admin can open Admins (tabs: Users, Profiles, Appearance, Backups) and use Backups to press Backup now, Download a copy to keep on a computer, or Restore. Restore first shows what would change, and takes a safety backup before it does anything. A yellow bar at the top warns the Admin if a backup failed. If the Admin has set up email, the email section of Backups can also send a copy of the backup and the complete Word export to a mailbox on a schedule. Appearance renames the Home page and the diary and chooses icons or words for the header bar.

Export to Word

Press Export (a downward arrow) in the header. Choose the whole diary in one Word file, one file per Subject, one Subject, or the page you are looking at (everyone who uses the profile can do all of these), or the Offline copy, one file you open in a web browser with no internet, with a search box. It is read-only: you cannot edit in it, and you download it again to refresh it. The Offline copy can also be made for one Subject or one page. It is not sent with the scheduled email backups. Also, on a Subject, Topic or Sub-topic, the header's Export opens the page ready to download exactly that. Flags are included unless you untick them. The start of the file name (the same for everyone using the profile) is set by Editors in the Data section of the diary Settings; the date at the end is your own choice on My settings. The file downloads to your computer. Exporting never changes the diary.

For Admins: the secret Offline link. On Admins > Profiles > Offline link you can make a link that downloads the Offline copy of a profile for anyone who has it, without signing in. This is handy for reading the diary on a phone where the network is poor. There is no password, so treat the link like a key and share it only with people you trust. The copy is remade every night, so it may be up to a day old; press Make a copy now to refresh it at once. To stop it working, press Switch off; to replace it, press Make a new link, and the old link stops working. A visitor can download 20 times an hour; resuming an interrupted download has its own, larger allowance.

Import from Word

The Admin can load a Word diary with Import from Word in the Data section of the diary Settings. It always shows a practice run first and changes nothing until the Admin approves it. It only goes into an empty profile.

Still stuck? Ask the Admin, and say which page you were on and what you pressed.
