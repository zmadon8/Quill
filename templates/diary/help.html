Help

This page explains the everyday tasks in plain words. Nothing here needs any technical knowledge. Use the Home button (the little house, top right) at any time to start again; Alt+H (Option+H on a Mac) does the same. The magnifier beside it is on every page and opens a search bar (on the Search page it jumps to the search box). The keyboard shortcuts are ⌥F for Search and ⌥H for Home (Option on a Mac; Alt on Windows; on Windows, Alt+F may also try to open the browser menu, so use Ctrl+Alt+F if so). Search can show results as cards or, when it covers the whole diary, "By Subject" with the Subjects on the left; "Search headers" looks only in Subject, Topic, Sub-topic and Section names.

Getting around

After you sign in you arrive on Home, which has one card for each program you may use: the {{ diary_name }}, the {{ fees_name }} and, if it has been given to you, {{ investments_name }}. Press a card to open that program. The little house always brings you back to Home. The magnifier opens a search bar; on Home it searches every program you can open. The gear opens Settings, and your initials (the round badge) open Account and Profile View. Remember me on the login page keeps you signed in for 30 days on your own device. Which page opens after you sign in, and which page each program opens on, is your choice (see Settings, below).

Settings

Settings is one row of tabs under the header. From Home the gear opens Start Screens; from inside a program it opens that program's own tab. Every tab with more than one page lists its pages down the left, and every page is built the same way as the Fee Register's Settings: a card for each section, a line saying what it is for, one row for each setting with a small tag that says who it affects, and a Save at the foot of the card (a note warns you about unsaved changes before you leave).

The tags: Only you (kept for your own account), Everyone in this profile (shared by everyone who uses that profile, so only Editors can change it), Everyone (the whole site) and This browser (kept in the browser you are using).

  • Users & Profiles (Admin): Users (people, roles, passwords), Profiles (notebooks, their names, the Offline link) and Profile Access.
  • Account and Profile View: your email address (used by Forgot my password), the letters in your badge, Change my password, and the profiles you can switch between.
  • Backup (Admin): Saved Backups and Email Backups (see Backups and restore).
  • Display: My Display (justify text, commas while typing numbers, Search as a word, Feebook grouping, the memo PDF button, the name of the memo's Word button, colour theme), My Home Page (the figures and extras Home shows) and, for an Admin, Names (the names of the site, Home and the programs).
  • Start Screens: what opens after sign-in, and the first page of each program.
  • {{ diary_name }}: Reading, My Number Formats (how Topics, Sub-topics, Sections, Items and several citations are numbered, how citations look, and the date in file names) and Data (the Word export file name, and for an Admin the Word import and the Offline link). Only Editors change what the profile shares; Readers can look.
  • {{ fees_name }}: Memos (default fees, memo text, party types), Payments (TDS, overdue days, modes, banks), Word Documents (file names, the Word template and signature, PDF through Microsoft 365), Years And Statistics, Display (figures, lists, your items per page) and Data (Excel backup, import, clear).

Finding a setting

Every settings page has a Find a setting box at the right of the Settings tabs. Type a word (for example password, colour, overdue or backup) and press Find. You get a list of every matching setting that you are allowed to see, with what it does, where it is, who it affects and a link that opens its page. On that page the list narrows as you type. With the box empty the page is the full list of all settings, grouped by tab, followed (for an Admin) by the settings that live on the server. The long reference for the person who looks after the site is docs/SETTINGS.md.

Reading and finding things (Case Law Diary)

On Home press the diary button. The Subjects page lists your Subjects. Press a Subject to read it. On a page showing just one Topic or Sub-topic, the two arrows beside its heading go to the previous and next one (from the last Sub-topic of a Topic, on to the next Topic). When a page reloads (after a change, Undo, or a phone reloading the tab) it opens where you were, not at the top. Use Search (top bar) to find words, a citation such as 2018 9 SCC 49, or an exact phrase in quotation marks. The ticks in Search (Citations, Flags, Headers) are remembered by this browser; History, at the start of that line, lists your recent searches. The Flags tab in the top bar (the flag) lists every flagged Item and Section, or one kind of flag, grouped by Subject, and searches the text beside the flags; its Hyperlinks page lists the citations that have a link, or those that do not yet. Group by Subject, beside the number of results, shows the results grouped under their Subjects or in one list; switching it does not change the search.

Editing

Only Editors can edit. Press the Edit link (the pencil) to switch editing on; the bar across the top says Editing. Every row then has two buttons: + Add and Edit. Change the text, press Save, and it is kept. An Item or a Section can be given a Flag (Pending, Overruled, Doubted or Other, chosen from a list, with text beside it if you like) as it is added, the same as when it is edited, on a phone as well. In Move to, press the arrow beside a Subject or Topic to open it (Expand all opens every one). Edit, then More, also has Promote to ... (Section to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Topic, Topic to Subject) and Demote to ... (Subject to Topic, Topic to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Section). A page says what will happen first, and Undo reverses it. A citation can carry a Link (a web address starting with https://), kept exactly as you type it; in the text the citation is then underlined and opens the link in a new tab. Settings, then Case Law Diary and Reading, has a Hyperlinks on citations tick box that turns links off and on, without reloading the page. Press Done when you have finished. If you only want to read, leave Edit off.

If someone else changed the same record first, nothing is lost: you see both versions side by side and choose Keep mine, Keep theirs or Edit and merge. Every saved version stays in History (Edit, then More, then History) and can be brought back.

Trash and Undo

Deleting never removes anything at once. It moves the record, with what is under it, to the Trash. Open Trash from the top bar and press Restore to bring it back in its old place. Only an explicit Delete permanently removes it for good. While editing, the Undo button reverses your last action.

Search

Type a word or part of a word. Capital letters and punctuation do not matter. Put exact phrases in quotation marks. You can narrow a search by Subject and see the results in context.

Search understands a few simple operators (the Search tips panel under the search box lists them):

  • fraud forgery: both words (AND is the default).
  • fraud OR forgery: either word. Write OR, AND and NOT in capital letters.
  • fraud -criminal or fraud NOT criminal: leaves out results that have the word.
  • "scc 49": the exact phrase. (fraud OR forgery) -criminal: brackets group words.
  • arbitra*: words that start with these letters.
  • SCC 2016: finds citations holding those words and numbers.
  • subject:Arbitration fraud: only in Subjects with that name (subject:"Civil Procedure" for several words).
  • flag:pending, flag:review (overruled or doubted) or flag:other: only flagged records of that kind.

If a search cannot be understood (for example a bracket that is never closed), it is run as plain words and a short note says so. Words you left out are not highlighted.

{{ fees_name }}

The {{ fees_name }} keeps your Firms and their Matters, the fee Memos you raise, and the Payments you receive. Its header has Statistics (the Dashboard and the Statistics tables, if switched on), Memos, Firms, Feebook (the payments ledger), Outstanding (what is still owed) and History. The magnifier searches firms, matters and memos.

A memo. Press New Memo (or New memo based on this on an existing one), choose the firm and matter, and the form fills in the matter's usual details and your defaults from Settings. The fee lines for Appearances, Conferences and Drafts / Misc are always on the form; leave a count at 0 for a line you do not need. Tick Custom beside an amount only to type a figure that is not count times fee. The total, and the total in words, update as you type and are worked out again when you save. Save as Draft keeps a memo with no number (the Drafts list shows them); Save gives it the next number of its financial year, or your own number if you tick Custom beside the memo number.

Word and PDF. A saved memo shows a Word button (it reads Save & Download if you chose that in Settings > {{ fees_name }} > Display) that downloads the memo made from the Word template in Settings > {{ fees_name }} > Word Documents. The same button at the foot of the memo form saves first and then downloads. When the Admin has connected Microsoft 365 there is also a PDF button; you can switch your own off in Settings > {{ fees_name }} > Display. The file name follows the pattern chosen in Word Documents.

Payments. On a memo press Add payment, or tick several memos on a list and pay them together. A payment can carry a TDS amount, a mode (from your Settings list) and a bank account. A memo shows as Paid, Unpaid, Overdue (when overdue is switched on in Settings) or Written Off (when write-offs are switched on), following from its payments. The Feebook lists payments; Outstanding lists what is owed, by firm and matter.

Data. Settings > {{ fees_name }} > Data has the Excel backup (one file holding every firm, matter, memo and payment), and for an Admin the backup, restore and clear page and the import of memos from Excel. These are in addition to the site-wide backups.

Profiles and users

A profile is a separate notebook of diary entries. A person may be given one or several profiles, as Reader or Editor in each. With several, the small arrow next to the name at the top of the diary (or the buttons on the Home page) switches between them; the one used last opens next time. The Admin makes profiles under Settings, tab Users & Profiles, page Profiles, and, on its Users page, adds people, changes passwords and chooses their profiles and roles. A deleted user can be restored from Deleted users on that tab for 30 days. Roles: Reader reads only, Editor reads and edits, Admin does everything including users and backups. If you see "No profile assigned", ask the Admin.

Backups and restore

The site saves a backup every night and keeps them for at least 30 days. The Admin can open Settings and its Backup tab to press Backup now, Download a copy to keep on a computer, or Restore. Restore first shows what would change, and takes a safety backup before it does anything. A yellow bar at the top warns the Admin if a backup failed. If the Admin has set up email, the email section of Backup can also send a copy of the backup and the complete Word export to a mailbox on a schedule. The Backup tab also shows the optional off-site copy. The guide for the person who looks after the site (backups, restoring, moving to a new host) is docs/MAINTENANCE.md. Display, page Names, renames the Home page and the programs.

Export to Word

Press Export in the header. Choose the whole diary in one Word file, one file per Subject, one Subject, or the page you are looking at (everyone who uses the profile can do all of these), or the Offline copy, one file you open in a web browser with no internet, with a search box. It is read-only: you cannot edit in it, and you download it again to refresh it. The Offline copy can also be made for one Subject or one page. It is not sent with the scheduled email backups. Also, on a Subject, Topic or Sub-topic, the header's Export opens the page ready to download exactly that. Flags are included unless you untick them. The start of the file name (the same for everyone using the profile) is set by Editors in the Data section of the diary Settings; the date at the end is your own choice on My settings. The file downloads to your computer. Exporting never changes the diary.

For Admins: the secret Offline link. On Admins > Profiles > Offline link you can make a link that downloads the Offline copy of a profile for anyone who has it, without signing in. This is handy for reading the diary on a phone where the network is poor. There is no password, so treat the link like a key and share it only with people you trust. The copy is remade every night, so it may be up to a day old; press Make a copy now to refresh it at once. To stop it working, press Switch off; to replace it, press Make a new link, and the old link stops working. A visitor can download 20 times an hour; resuming an interrupted download has its own, larger allowance.

Import from Word

The Admin can load a Word diary with Import from Word in the Data section of the diary Settings. It always shows a practice run first and changes nothing until the Admin approves it. It only goes into an empty profile.

{{ investments_name }}

{{ investments_name }} is a separate program for recording purchases and sales of shares, mutual funds and other investments. It appears on Home only for people who have been given an Investments profile, and only once the Admin has switched it on. The Short / Full choice in Settings > Display shows every rupee amount either in short figures, the same way as the Fee Register (for example ₹2.41 cr, ₹24.13 lac, ₹85.4k), or in full (₹24,12,600); pointing at a short figure shows the exact amount, and Excel exports always contain the exact figures. Settings > Display sets how many decimals units and full rupee figures show (short figures always show up to 2). To start: add a broker (Settings > Brokers), add an investment (Holdings > Investments > Add investment), then record a purchase or an opening balance. To load trades you already have, use Import a Statement (Holdings > Transactions > More, beside Opening Balance): a CSV or Excel file from your broker or fund house, reviewed line by line before anything is saved; a sheet of your existing holdings works too (date, name, code, type, folio, portfolio, broker, buy or sell, price, quantity; the page offers a sample sheet). Investments, portfolios and brokers the program does not have yet are added when you save, and listed first so you can check them. To start again, an Admin can clear the profile's transaction history in Settings > General; a backup is taken first. A sale can never be more than you hold with the chosen broker and folio; if an edit would break a later sale it is refused and the sale is shown so you can fix it first. A purchase or sale whose details are not known yet is saved with Mark as Tentative ticked: only the date, investment, portfolio and broker are needed, and an amount may be given. A tentative purchase counts at cost (or is left out, as Settings says) and a tentative sale is ignored; neither is in the first-in-first-out matching. Finalise it later by editing it and unticking the box, or from To complete. Transactions can show only the tentative ones. Every change is logged in History, where a deleted entry can be brought back. Prices are fetched every night (and with the refresh button Update Prices in the header); a price you type yourself is never overwritten by a fetched one. Everyone can see the shared investments and their price history in Settings > Catalogue and on each investment's page, where an Editor can correct a price, fetch the history again from a chosen date, or remove the prices from before the first purchase; an Admin keeps the shared details and structural changes, and can upload years of history once in Settings > Price sources, and download or email a price archive. Reports has Performance (your XIRR against the Default Index over the same cash flows, and Alpha, the difference), Charts (with an optional 50 or 200-day moving average beside every line, to compare an investment's trend with the index's), Total Portfolio Value on any date and the Annual Report for each financial year with short and long-term gains. In Settings > {{ investments_name }} > Display > Home page figures, an Editor chooses the figures the Overview shows, including a "Returns by period" table for the whole history and the last 5, 3 and 1 years. Settings > Indices adds indices (by their Yahoo symbol) and chooses each investment's sector index. Record dividends under Holdings > Dividends; they never count in portfolio value and count in XIRR only when Include dividends in XIRR is ticked in Settings > General. The Period drop-down under the Overview cards chooses the period behind Gain, Portfolio XIRR, the index XIRR and Alpha there, and Portfolio XIRR on Home (your own choice). A split or bonus is recorded once for everyone and changes your holdings only after you apply it on Corporate actions. Set alert thresholds in Alert settings on the Alerts page and guardrails on single holdings on the Alerts page. Tax and gains figures are a record-keeping aid, not tax advice: check them with your CA.

Return, CAGR and XIRR

{{ investments_name }} shows three different measures, and every figure says which one it is.

  • Return is the plain gain since the money went in, as a percentage. It is not scaled to a year. On an investment's page a lot younger than a year shows it with the days it covers, for example "4.6% over 180 days".
  • CAGR is the compound growth a year between two dates, from one start value and one end value. A lot a year old or more shows its return this way, for example "9.3% CAGR". The Performance page can also show the CAGR of an investment's own price.
  • XIRR is the yearly rate that fits every dated purchase, sale and the closing value. It is the figure on Holdings, the Overview, the reports, the Annual Report and the Home card. Alpha is your XIRR less the index's XIRR on the same cash flows.

On Holdings, XIRR Alpha is the XIRR less the Index XIRR. CAGR is the investment's own price growth a year from the day its first unit was bought to its latest price, Index CAGR is the Default Index's growth over exactly the same dates, and CAGR Alpha is the difference.

The Overview's "change over the period" and the Year-by-year "Index change over the year" are plain changes, not yearly rates, so do not compare them directly with XIRR.

If something goes wrong

  • I cannot sign in. After 5 wrong passwords a name is blocked for 15 minutes. Use Forgot my password if you have saved an email address; otherwise the Admin sets a new password on the Users page.
  • "No profile assigned", or a page says I am not allowed. The Admin has not given you a profile in that program, or has given you a role that only reads (Settings > Users & Profiles).
  • A setting is missing. Use Find a setting. A setting for a program you cannot open, or one only an Admin may change, is not listed for you.
  • The memo PDF button is missing or fails. Check Display > Memo PDF button, then ask the Admin to look at Settings > {{ fees_name }} > Word Documents > PDF export. The Word button always works.
  • A saved change is not there. Someone else may have changed the same record first (the clash screen says so), or the record may be in the Trash.
  • The yellow bar at the top. The last backup failed or is more than 36 hours old. Tell the Admin.

Still stuck? Ask the Admin, and say which page you were on and what you pressed.
