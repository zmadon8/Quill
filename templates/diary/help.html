Help

This page explains the everyday tasks in plain words. Nothing here needs any technical knowledge. Use the Home button (the little house, top right) at any time to start again. The magnifier beside it is on every page and opens a search bar (on the Search page it jumps to the search box). The keyboard shortcuts are ⌥F for Search and ⌥H for Home (Option on a Mac; Alt on Windows; on Windows, Alt+F may also try to open the browser menu, so use Ctrl+Alt+F if so). Search can show results as cards or, when it covers the whole diary, "By Subject" with the Subjects on the left; "Search headers" looks only in Subject, Topic, Sub-topic and Section names.

Reading and finding things

On Home press the diary button. The Subjects page lists your Subjects. Press a Subject to read it. On a page showing just one Topic or Sub-topic, the two arrows beside its heading go to the previous and next one (from the last Sub-topic of a Topic, on to the next Topic). Use Search (top bar) to find words, a citation such as 2018 9 SCC 49, or an exact phrase in quotation marks. Tick For review only in Search to list everything marked For review. The ticks in Search (Citations only, For review only, Search headers) are remembered by this browser. Group by Subject, beside the number of results, shows the results grouped under their Subjects or in one list; switching it does not change the search.

Editing

Only Editors can edit. Press the Edit link (the pencil) to switch editing on; the bar across the top says Editing. Every row then has two buttons: + Add and Edit. Change the text, press Save, and it is kept. An Item can be given a Flag and a For review mark as it is added, the same as when it is edited, on a phone as well. In Move to, press the arrow beside a Subject or Topic to open it (Expand all opens every one). Edit, then More, also has Promote to ... (Section to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Topic, Topic to Subject) and Demote to ... (Subject to Topic, Topic to Sub-topic, Sub-topic to Section). A page says what will happen first, and Undo reverses it. Press Done when you have finished. If you only want to read, leave Edit off.

If someone else changed the same record first, nothing is lost: you see both versions side by side and choose Keep mine, Keep theirs or Edit and merge. Every saved version stays in History (Edit, then More, then History) and can be brought back.

Trash and Undo

Deleting never removes anything at once. It moves the record, with what is under it, to the Trash. Open Trash from the top bar and press Restore to bring it back in its old place. Only an explicit Delete permanently removes it for good. While editing, the Undo button reverses your last action.

Search

Type a word or part of a word. Capital letters and punctuation do not matter. Put exact phrases in quotation marks. You can narrow a search by Subject and see the results in context.

Settings

My settings (every user) chooses your email address (so you can use Forgot my password on the login page), what opens after you log in and how numbers look. Tick Remember me when logging in to stay signed in for 30 days on your own device. Settings (in the diary menu) sets the name shown at the top and the name used for Word files; only Editors can change them. The Admin also has Settings on Home to rename the Home page and the diary.

Profiles and users

A profile is a separate notebook of diary entries. Each person is linked to one profile and sees only that one. The Admin makes profiles under Profiles, and adds people, changes passwords and chooses roles under Users. Roles: Reader reads only, Editor reads and edits, Admin does everything including users and backups. If you see "No profile assigned", ask the Admin.

Backups and restore

The site saves a backup every night and keeps them for at least 30 days. The Admin can open Backups to press Backup now, Download a copy to keep on a computer, or Restore. Restore first shows what would change, and takes a safety backup before it does anything. A yellow bar at the top warns the Admin if a backup failed. If the Admin has set up email, Backups, Email backups can also send a copy of the backup and the complete Word export to a mailbox on a schedule.

Export to Word

Open Export in the top bar. Choose the whole diary in one Word file, one file per Subject, or just the page you are looking at. On a Subject, Topic or Sub-topic the Export button at the top downloads that page; on a computer its small arrow opens a menu with This page, Full diary and By Subject (Editors and the Admin), and on a phone the button is a download arrow. Readers can export the page only. Review markers are included. The file downloads to your computer. Exporting never changes the diary.

Import from Word

The Admin can load a Word diary with Import on Home. It always shows a practice run first and changes nothing until the Admin approves it. It only goes into an empty profile.

Still stuck? Ask the Admin, and say which page you were on and what you pressed.
