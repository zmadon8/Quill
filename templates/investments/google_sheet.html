Google Sheet prices

A Google Sheet looks up the closing prices of your shares and indices with GOOGLEFINANCE and sends them here every evening. Its prices fill only days that have no price yet; the nightly Yahoo and bhavcopy prices (the exchange's official close) replace them. Mutual funds are not in the sheet: their NAVs come from AMFI's daily file. The sheet receives only codes and names, never holdings or transactions.

Set it up once

  1. On Price sources, under Local helper, make a token (label it, for example, Google Sheet). It is shown only once: keep it to hand.
  2. Make a new Google Sheet. Choose File > Settings: set the time zone to (GMT+05:30) India Standard Time, and on the Calculation tab set recalculation to On change and every hour.
  3. Choose Extensions > Apps Script, delete what is there, paste the script below, and save.
  4. Reload the sheet. A Quill menu appears. Choose, in turn: 1. Set the token (Google asks you to allow the script the first time), 2. Refresh the list of investments, and Switch on the daily timers.
  5. Check the sheet: every row should show a price within a minute. Where the Google ticker is wrong or empty, type the right one in column C (for example NSE:RELIANCE, BOM:500325, INDEXNSE:NIFTY_50); your corrections are kept when the list is refreshed. Then choose 3. Send prices now and look at column H.

The timers refresh the list at 7 am and send prices at 7 pm and 11 pm, India time. If a run fails, Google emails the sheet's owner, and the run shows on Price sources.
