Indices

Every investment is compared with the Default Index (Settings > General). Switch on "Use a sectoral index as well" there to compare each investment with its own sector index too. Indices are shared by every profile. An index's prices are fetched every night while it is in use (as a Default Index or a sector index in any profile); a newly chosen one gets 10 years of history that night.
