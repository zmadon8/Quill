Corporate actions

An action is recorded once for everyone, because it is a fact about the company. It changes this profile's holdings only after you confirm it, and your original trades are never changed. Each action is decided case by case:

  • Bonus or split. Choose who adds the units. Automatic: the program adds them (a split multiplies the units and keeps the cost and purchase dates; a bonus adds a lot at no cost from the ex-date). I record the units as transactions: you record or import them as an entry with the origin Bonus or Split (no cost, so a split's new units start their own holding period; check with your CA). Create the transactions for me makes those entries now for you to check. The two ways never mix for one action, so units are not counted twice.
  • Demerger. The parent's lots lose the stated percentage of their cost, for the calculations only, and the new company's shares you record (origin "Received on a demerger") take that cost. Applying it also enters those shares for you (the ratio gives the units, at a cost of 0) for every portfolio and broker that held the parent; check them against your statement. A cost percentage of 0 leaves all the cost with the parent's shares. You can edit the action later, and the shares the program entered follow. Choose whether those shares keep the parent shares' purchase dates (off unless you switch it on); confirm the percentage and the dates with your CA. Both costs are shown beside the cost from your original transactions.
