Corporate actions

An action is recorded once for everyone, because it is a fact about the company. It changes this profile's holdings only after you confirm it; your original trades are never changed. A split multiplies the units and keeps the cost and purchase dates; a bonus adds units at zero cost from the allotment date (check this treatment with your CA). Mergers and demergers: record them as "other" and correct the lots by hand (cost override on the purchase).
