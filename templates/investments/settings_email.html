Email reports

Emails a short report on this profile to the people you choose, each by name, once each ticked period ends: the week (Monday to Sunday), the month, the quarter, or the financial year (1 April to 31 March). Each email gives the growth over the period against the Default Index, the market value of the profile and of each portfolio, the purchases, sales, capital gains and interest income of the period, and attaches an Excel sheet of the holdings and every transaction. The annual email also attaches the Annual Report for the year. The nightly job (about 02:00 IST) sends whatever is due.
