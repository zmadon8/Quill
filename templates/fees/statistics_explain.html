The basics

Which memos count
A memo counts in the month of its memo date and in the financial year it belongs to (1 April to 31 March). Draft memos are left out, unless the page is asked to include them; they are then counted in the current month.
Which payments count
Amount Received and Received + TDS follow the date of the payment, not the date of the memo it paid. Amount Recovered follows the memo: it is what has been paid against the memos issued in the period, whenever it was paid.
Entered by hand
In Settings, Start Years & Statistics, each month can have its working days, extra appearances, conferences and drafts, and figures that replace the worked-out ones. Extras are added to the memo counts. A replacement figure is used instead of the worked-out one; the table shows it like any other figure.

Working days completed

Every rate divides by the working days completed, never by calendar days. The working days of a month are the ones entered for it in Settings, Start Years & Statistics.

A past month
Its entered working days, in full.
The current month
Entered working days × (today’s date ÷ days in the month), rounded to a whole day. Example: 20 working days in November, on 19 November: 20 × 19 ÷ 30 = 12.7, so 13.
A future month
0.
A quarter or a financial year
The sum of its months, worked out as above.
The last 30 days
From the day after today’s date last month up to today (on 19 November: 20 October to 19 November). Each month in it gives its entered working days × (its days inside the window ÷ its days in all), and the total is rounded to a whole day. Example, with 22 working days entered for October and 20 for November: 22 × 12 ÷ 31 = 8.5, plus 20 × 19 ÷ 30 = 12.7, total 21.2, so 21.
No working days entered
The rate shows 0.

Projected Totals

For the current financial year only, each total is projected to 31 March at the pace so far:

Projected for the year = so far ÷ working days completed so far × working days in the whole financial year

Which totals
Amount Billed, Amount Received, Received + TDS, Appearances, Conferences, Drafts and, on the Dashboard, Memos. Received is projected from its own pace (by payment date). Rates (Fees / Day, the “/ Week” figures, % Recovered) are averages already and are not projected; neither is Amount Recovered.
Working days in the year
The working days entered for all twelve months. A month with none entered counts 0, in the year and in the days completed.
Month by month
A month already over: what happened. The current month: what happened so far + the rate a working day × its working days still to come. A future month: the rate a working day × its working days. These add up to the projected year.
Example
On 19 November, with 230 working days entered for the year and 145 completed (132 from April to October, and 20 × 19 ÷ 30 = 13 in November): billed so far ₹23.90 L is ₹16,483 a working day, so the projected year is ₹16,483 × 230 = ₹37.91 L. November is projected at its ₹2.10 L so far + ₹16,483 × 7 = ₹3.25 L; December (16 working days) at ₹2.64 L.
Where it shows
Dashboard: under each “This Year” box, and hatched on top of the bars in Billed by month. Statistics > Standard: Show By Month draws the running total, solid so far and dashed to 31 March; Show By Financial Year draws the current year as one stacked column, so far and the projected rest of the year. Statistics > Explore: Show As Projected Totals, with Rows by Month or by Financial Year. Point at a chart (or tap it, or use the arrow keys) to see the actual and the projected figures of that month or year.
When nothing shows
Until at least one working day of the year is completed.

Year End

Totals for one financial year: memos, billed, received, TDS, written off, new matters, active firms, appearances, and Appearances / Week (appearances ÷ working days completed in the year × 5).
